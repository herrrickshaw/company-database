# Sources and licences
Check each provider's current terms before redistributing the data. Attribution is required by most of them.

| Source | URL | Licence (as understood) |
|---|---|---|
| GLEIF Golden Copy | https://www.gleif.org/lei-data/gleif-golden-copy | CC0 1.0 |
| UK Companies House basic company data | https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_output.html | Open Government Licence v3.0 |
| France SIRENE (INSEE) | https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/base-sirene-des-entreprises-et-de-leurs-etablissements-siren-siret | Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0 |
| Norway Enhetsregisteret | https://data.brreg.no/enhetsregisteret/ | NLOD 2.0 |
| Estonia e-Business Register open data | https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/downloading-open-data | open data; terms not verified here |
| Damodaran company list | NYU Stern, pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar | public academic dataset, no formal licence; cite |
| India IEM-linked CINs | DPIIT IEM Part A/B filings, matched to MCA records | derived; not redistributed here |
| Defence-industry registry | national registries and industry-association member lists | compiled by the author; check before making public |
